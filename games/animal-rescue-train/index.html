THE LITTLE RESCUE LINE

Animal Rescue Train

A little room. A safe way home.

No timer. Take your time.

How to play

  1. Choose a queue head. Preview the next three animals; only the front animal can board.
  2. Choose a carriage. Each has three seats. Plan your groups and keep room for the next arrival.
  3. Three of a kind depart. Rescue all three animals and get an empty carriage immediately.

Mixed groups cannot leave or be rearranged. Every animal is needed, so a mixed group requires undo or a retry.

Rescue every target to win. If no passenger can board before the targets are met, the puzzle ends.
